Emotion Detection for Customer Feedback

Aaryan Patel and Ivan Rodriguez (Brown University). Student research paper, Inspirit AI, 2024.

Surveys miss how people feel in the moment, so we trained models to read facial emotion as a feedback signal. A focal-loss model reached 92.05% test accuracy on 5-class color images, up from 76% with ResNet50V2 transfer learning, and a 4-block CNN reached 83.11% on FER+ grayscale. Run on movie clips across star ratings, detected sadness fell from 20% to 12% as ratings rose.

Full paper available on request.
